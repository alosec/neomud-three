# Town Horizon Batching Pass

Date: 2026-05-29

## Change

Reworked the Town Square painted horizon from four separate backdrop systems into one shared batched horizon system.

The new horizon keeps the same four-sided outdoor coverage, but batches the far moss terraces, dark tree band, near foliage, quiet background buildings, roof hints, and ground edge into shared instanced meshes. This gives the plaza edges more layered landscape depth while reducing draw-call pressure.

## Budget

Town Square full offline smoke moved from `238/240` draw calls to `224/240` draw calls. The room is still triangle-hot at `78868/80000`, so future Town Square work should reclaim or simplify geometry before adding more visible objects.

## Verdict

Accepted as a production-direction pass. It does not make the outdoor world final, but it removes some of the flat-sky cutoff problem and creates budget headroom without broadening room scope.
